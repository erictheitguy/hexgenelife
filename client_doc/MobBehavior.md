# Mob Behavior

Mobs should have the ability to have different behaviors.

Some of the behavoirs taken would be packs or herds taken from real life. The mob should have modifiable method of its instinct to form groups or prefer a solo life.

This also applies to how a mob might hunter another mob. Will it lay in ambush or will it charge towards another mob in attempt to kill it.

Since mobs age and die any behavior the tends to keep the mob from breeding and producing offspring should naturally remmove that behavior. There shouldnt need to be any hard coded behavior other than a hopefully natural desire to breed. The frequency and overall drive to breed can be behavior.
